## What is in here — and what is not
- In here: procedures, roles, partners, decisions, shared knowledge.
- Not in here: anything about a named employee beyond "currently holds
  role X" — no performance, conduct, health or salary. Personal notes
  belong in a private vault, not a shared one.
- `confidentiality:` in the frontmatter is a LABEL, not access control.
  Real separation only comes from separate vaults with separate access.

## How to trust what you read
- `status: stable` + a filled `verified:` = a human confirmed this.
- `generated:` = an AI wrote it and nobody has checked it yet.
- No `verified:` and something is at stake? Ask the `owner:` named in
  the note before acting on it.
- `stale_after:` in the past, or `review_due:` overdue → treat as
  outdated until someone re-checks it.

## Rhythm
- Weekly review: `00-inbox/` and `00-inbox/suggestions/` go to zero.
- Every note has an `owner:` — content without an owner rots.
