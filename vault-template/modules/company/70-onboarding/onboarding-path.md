# Onboarding path — first hour to day 90

Welcome to {{COMPANY}}. This is the path everyone walks. Your role adds
its own plan on top (see [index.md](index.md)) — this file is the part
that is the same for all of us.

## First hour — you change something before you read everything

1. **Improve one note.** Open any note in this vault, find one thing
   that is wrong, missing or confusing for a newcomer, and write the
   correction as a file in `00-inbox/suggestions/`
   (`YYYY-MM-DD-your-topic.md`, a few sentences are plenty).
   This is task number one on purpose. You are the only person who will
   ever read this vault with fresh eyes, and that view expires within a
   week. It also teaches you the whole workflow in ten minutes:
   suggestions are proposals, a human releases them, released content
   gets `status: stable` and a `verified:` entry. Nobody gets this
   wrong — a rejected suggestion costs nothing.
2. Read `index.md` in the vault root: which folder holds what.
3. Read your own role note in `60-roles/`. Anything unclear in it → same
   route as step 1.

## First week — the ground you stand on

- Work through the SOPs in `50-processes/` that your role note lists
  under "Needs to know". Try one of them with someone watching.
- Meet the roles you depend on — the people, but read the ROLE notes:
  they tell you what is actually owed to whom.
- Skim `40-decisions/` for the records that still shape daily work: why
  things are the way they are saves you from proposing them again.
- Second suggestion due: after a week of real work you see different
  gaps than on day one.

## First 30 days — do it, don't watch it

- Run your recurring tasks unassisted, with the SOP open.
- Where an SOP does not match reality: the SOP is wrong until proven
  otherwise → suggestion. Reality is the source, the note is the copy.
- Know where things are written down: contracts, partners
  (`80-partners/`), who decides what.
- Checkpoint with your role's owner: what still needs a second pair of
  eyes?

## Day 60 — cover someone

- Stand in for at least one absence listed under "Cover" in your role
  note. Standing in is the honest test of whether the notes are usable.
- Own one small improvement end to end: notice it, write it, get it
  released, watch someone else use it.

## Day 90 — you are the one who explains it

- Walk one procedure through with the next newcomer, or write the SOP
  for something that only lives in your head.
- Review your own role note against the last three months and correct
  it — by now you know it better than the person who wrote it.
- Closing question with your role's owner: what do you still not know
  that you would need if everyone else were away tomorrow?
