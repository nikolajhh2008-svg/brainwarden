# 60-contribution

What you actually did, with evidence, while you still remember it. One
file per year, appended weekly.

**Rules here:** Facts with a source, not self-assessment. `impact: "sync
cut from 4 h to 12 min"` is worth nothing without `impact_source:
"log measurement 2026-08-07, n=6 runs"` — an unsourced number is just a
self-rating in disguise, and self-ratings are exactly what this folder
exists to replace. `ownership: private` on every note here: your record
of your work is yours, and stays yours when the job ends.
**Weekly, five minutes:** what shipped · what you reviewed · what you
designed or documented · who you helped · what broke and what you learned
from it. An empty week is a valid entry.
**NOT here:** the work itself (that is the project note) · opinions about
colleagues (never anywhere) · anything you could not show your manager.
