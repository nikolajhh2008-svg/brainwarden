# 30-knowledge/people

One note per person that matters to the human — a dossier, not an index
card.

**Rules here:** `type: person`. A note holds role and relationship,
confirmed facts, recent interactions, and open questions marked as open.
Separate what is confirmed from what is assumed: unconfirmed facts stay
under "Open" until someone actually checks them. No `source:` field —
where a fact came from belongs in the line it supports ("(said so
himself, 2026-01-15)"). Search before creating: one person = exactly one
note, however their name is spelled today (put spelling variants in
`aliases:`).
**NOT here:** this folder does not exist in `company` mode — dossiers
about employees are a data-protection problem, role descriptions are
not. There it is replaced by `60-roles/`.
