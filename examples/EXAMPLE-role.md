# Shift lead

**Purpose of the role:** without it, nobody on the floor can decide
anything between opening and closing, and every small problem waits for
the managing director.
**Currently held by:** Mira — one line, nothing further.

## Responsible for
- The shop opens on time and is staffed for the whole opening period
- Goods received during the shift are confirmed in writing the same day
- Every incident on the floor (customer complaint, damage, no-show) is
  written down before the shift ends

## Decides alone / has to ask
- Decides alone: shift swaps within the existing roster · goodwill
  refunds up to EUR 50 · closing 15 minutes early in an emergency
- Has to ask managing-director: anything above EUR 50, additional paid
  hours, sending someone home, any statement to a supplier about money

## Cover
- Absent: the senior floor staff on duty takes over opening, staffing
  and incident logging. Goodwill refunds do NOT transfer — those wait or
  go to the managing director.

  `50-processes/goods-receipt.md`
